# Development Roadmap

1. Foundation and settings.
2. Platform authentication and permissions.
3. Hospital registry and lifecycle.
4. Secure PostgreSQL tenant provisioning.
5. Tenant migration orchestration.
6. Hospital invitations, memberships, and tenant authorization.
7. Department templates and per-hospital configuration.
8. Healthcare modules: doctors, nurses, patients, appointments, clinic, pharmacy, diagnostics, billing, admission, blood bank, inventory.
9. Tenant isolation tests, backups, observability, deployment.
